About

Who I am

Functional Analyst with 3 years of ERP experience — SAP S/4HANA Public Cloud and Odoo. I work at the intersection of business and technical teams to turn complex operational processes into flows that actually work. My starting point: understand what's broken before proposing anything.

Three years across two very different ERP ecosystems — SAP at scale, Odoo in depth — have reinforced one conviction: the technical side of an implementation is rarely what determines its success. What matters is the conversation: understanding what a team actually does (not what the process map says they do), mapping where the system creates friction, and making the case for the standard clearly enough that people choose it over the workaround they built.

My approach

  • Fit-to-standard first — challenge requirements against the SAP or Odoo standard before accepting an exception.
  • Systematic AS-IS / TO-BE BPMN mapping — so that everyone, technical or not, understands the process.
  • Between business and technical — I translate in both directions without losing the nuance.
