About

Who I am

Functional Analyst with 3 years of ERP experience — SAP S/4HANA Public Cloud and Odoo. I work at the intersection of business and technical teams to turn complex operational processes into flows that actually work. My starting point: understand what's broken before proposing anything.

My approach

  • Fit-to-standard first — challenge requirements against the SAP or Odoo standard before accepting an exception. In practice: adapt the process to the tool, not the other way around.
  • Systematic AS-IS / TO-BE process mapping (BPMN diagrams) — so that everyone, technical or not, can follow the logic and spot what changes.
  • Between business and technical — I translate in both directions without losing the nuance.
