About

Who I am

Functional Analyst with 3 years across SAP S/4HANA and Odoo. I build the bridge between what business teams need and what technical systems can actually do — and I start by listening for where things break down.

What drives me

I build to solve, not to impress. When I watch a sales team bypass a system six times a day, that's not incompetence — it's a signal the process was broken from the start. I start there. I love turning invisible friction into flows that work on day one, flows people actually choose to use because they make their work easier, not harder.

Three years in two different ERP worlds taught me one thing: the implementation technology is almost never the bottleneck. It's the conversation. Understanding what a team actually does (vs. what the org chart says), mapping exactly where a system creates friction, and building a case for the standard that's compelling enough people prefer it to their workarounds.

My approach

  • Fit-to-standard first — challenge requirements against the SAP or Odoo standard before accepting an exception.
  • Systematic AS-IS / TO-BE BPMN mapping — so that everyone, technical or not, understands the process.
  • Between business and technical — I translate in both directions without losing the nuance.
